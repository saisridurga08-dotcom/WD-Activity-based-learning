Healthy Recipes

🥗 Vegetable Salad

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot and lemon.

Preparation: Wash and cut the vegetables. Mix them in a bowl and add lemon juice.

Estimated calories: 100 kcal per serving

🍎 Fruit Bowl

Ingredients: Apple, banana, grapes and papaya.

Preparation: Wash and cut the fruits. Mix them in a bowl and serve fresh.

Estimated calories: 150 kcal per serving

🥣 Oats Breakfast

Ingredients: Oats, milk and banana.

Preparation: Cook the oats with milk. Add banana pieces and serve.

Estimated calories: 250 kcal per serving
